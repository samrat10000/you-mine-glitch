## How to build something like it for the 5 O'CLOCK reel (original, not a copy)
1. Open with a solo piano only: bare fifth (low note plus its fifth), three or four quick soft notes, then let it ring. Close, intimate, sustain pedal down.
2. Keep one tempo around 65 BPM. Use sixteenth-note figures with an octave doubling, then leave a 1 to 2 second rest after each phrase so the room reverb carries it.
3. Move the same figure through three centres (home, one step that feels sad, one step that lifts), then return home. Do not make it a melody, make it a pattern that shifts.
4. Add the held bed (pipe-organ or soft low pad) under it after the first phrase, and a very quiet low pulse every two beats.
5. Dialogue and music should blend: place spoken lines in the long rests (the 1 to 2 s gaps are the natural spots), and keep the piano quiet under the voice so it never overlaps the words. Lower the piano 6 to 10 dB under speech and let it swell back in the next rest.
6. Mix: dark, rolled-off highs above 4 to 6 kHz, long reverb (3 to 5 s), steady level, end on an unresolved chord left to ring.
7. Use a real, licensed or self-played piano and a real organ sample. Sam wants nothing that reads as AI-made.
