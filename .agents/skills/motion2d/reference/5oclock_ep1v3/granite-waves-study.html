What the Granite Waves reel is, and how much of it we can copy

Short answer. It is a hand-drawn 2D vertical anime series (made for phones, 9:16), not 3D. The reel is Episode 5, part 1, "Mentor Zima", of Granite Waves by Tezeze. It looks expensive because of restraint: flat colour, soft blurred painted backgrounds, quiet acting and a very warm, low voice.

Recommendation. Copy the look in Blender with Grease Pencil on our existing pencil-sketch pipeline, and cast a real human voice. Both are within reach. Details at the end.

Frames from the reel

1. What art form this is

  • Name: indie 2D anime in a vertical "micro-drama" format. Flat cel-style colour with soft painted backgrounds.
  • What gives it the look: thin dark line art, two or three flat tones per surface, a desaturated palette with one accent colour, backgrounds blurred to fake lens depth, a light grain-free film grade, and subtitles burned in near the lower third.
  • Motion style: very limited. Long held poses, small head turns, eyelid and hair movement. This is why it looks smooth and costly while being cheap to make.

2. The channel

  • Account: granitewavesseries, 1.2M followers, verified, 98 posts. Creator account: tezeze_art, 630K followers.
  • Funding and release: Ryuu, a phone streaming app, funded the whole first season. Instagram is the trailer feed.
  • Other reels (views shown on the grid): the season welcome reel, 58.5M. A "Final vs animatic" behind-the-scenes reel for Ep 2 "Scenic route". "Where is your soul?" character reel. Several others between 0.7M and 15M. This reel: 933K views, 44.8K likes.
  • Same publisher, sibling series: Infinit and Sonnenlied, same app, same format, same credit style.

Voice recipe

  • Cast a real person with a low, tired, close-mic voice. You asked for outputs that never look or sound AI-made, and the reel's voice is real acting.
  • Direction: speak quietly, start lines with a little weight, let them fade. Leave a second of silence between lines.
  • Recording: close to the mic (about 10 cm), pop filter, quiet small room or blankets, no reverb.
  • Mix: cut below 70 Hz, gentle lift around 150 to 250 Hz, roll off above 6 to 8 kHz, light compression, no reverb, music ducked 8 to 10 dB under the lines.

Recommendation

  • Build one test shot first: a character in profile, held pose, blurred window background, one red accent, with a real voice line. Compare it to frame 1 above.
  • Keep key poses hand-drawn and use Blender for fills, camera, blur and grade.
  • Keep motion small and holds long. That is the main secret of this look.
  • Do not copy their characters, logo or title design. Use the technique only.
