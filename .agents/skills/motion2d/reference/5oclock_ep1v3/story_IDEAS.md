## B. 5 O'CLOCK v1 (16:29 IST), picture: images/6-chime.png
- Every town in Japan plays a chime at 5 PM to send children home. In the mountain/sea town of **Kiriyama** (fictional, 6,000 people, one train line), everyone who heard it vanished. **Sam, 17, had his headphones on.**
- Piles of clothes where people stood; phones inside still ring. Trains stopped. Calling Tokyo: "there is no town called Kiriyama".
- The chime plays again every day at 5:00. Anyone who hears it walks away forever.
- After dark the gone come back. They look normal, smile, wave.
- **Rules notebook** found at the koban (police box), left by **Officer Tanaka**, who survived 11 days:
  1. Be indoors, with your ears covered, at 5:00.
  2. If the speaker says your name, do not answer.
  3. Never pick up a ringing phone that belongs to one of the gone.
  4. Don't count the people at the bus stop. There is always one more.
  5. Nothing red outside.
  6. If someone from the town waves at you, don't wave back.
  7. (torn out)
- Mysteries: a new rule appears every night in Sam's own handwriting; someone else is breaking rules on purpose; season cliffhanger: 1964 newspaper "Kiriyama boy, only survivor", the boy looks exactly like Sam.
- Backups offered (not chosen): THE LIST (Japan bans 27 ordinary objects after one night; Sam's evacuated town still has them, he stays to find his sister); LOST & FOUND (anything found must be handed in at the koban by midnight; everyone who kept something is gone; Sam kept something).

## C. Merge with "other Earth" + kaiju (16:37 IST), picture: images/7-answer.png
One sentence: **Every day at 5 PM, Kiriyama's song gets an answer from another Earth, and every day what comes through is bigger.**
1. The song is a signal: the 5 PM loudspeakers broadcast on an old 1960s radio tower; it reaches another Earth.
2. The other Earth answers: 13 seconds after the chime ends, the sky plays the same song back, slightly wrong. Everyone who heard the answer was pulled across. The wavers are what came back in their place.
3. Every answer opens the door wider: the other Earth is overrun by kaiju; a crack in the sky over the mountains grows each day.
4. Sam is the only one who can close it, because he never heard the answer.
Suspense ladder:

| 1 | Nothing visible. Town gone. | Rule 1 |
| 3 | Speaker says his name in his mother's voice | The other side knows who is left |
| 7 | Crows with too many eyes | The crack is real and growing |
| 13 | House-sized footprint in the rice fields | Something big already walked through |
| 21 | Its roar from the fog on the mountain | It waits for the door to open fully |
| 30 | First full kaiju attack on Kiriyama | Rule 7 was how to close the door |
